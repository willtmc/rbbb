# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in RBBB a harassment-free experience for
everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex
characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience,
education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race,
religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming,
diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Expected behavior

- Demonstrate empathy and kindness.
- Respect differing viewpoints and operational experience.
- Give and accept constructive feedback.
- Focus disagreement on ideas and observable consequences.
- Take responsibility, apologize, and learn from mistakes.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, sexualized attention, sustained
disruption, and publication of another person's private information are not
acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior privately to the maintainers through GitHub's
private vulnerability reporting channel, with “Code of Conduct” in the report
title. Maintainers will review reports promptly, protect reporter privacy as
far as practical, and choose a response proportionate to the conduct, up to
temporary or permanent exclusion from project spaces.

This policy is informed by the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1.
