# Governance

RBBB is in a founder-led incubation phase.

## Roles

- **Maintainer:** reviews contributions, accepts RFCs, manages releases, and
  protects the project's security and compatibility guarantees.
- **Contributor:** submits code, documentation, scenarios, reviews, or domain
  knowledge under the project license and contribution rules.

The initial maintainer is Will McLemore. Additional maintainers may be invited
based on sustained, constructive participation and sound judgment around
auction trust boundaries.

## Decisions

Routine fixes and non-normative documentation changes are decided through pull
request review. Changes to normative auction behavior require an RFC and a
recorded maintainer decision. Compatibility-breaking changes require an RFC,
migration guidance, and an appropriate major version.

Maintainers should seek broad input, especially from people who operate
auctions, while retaining a clear decision owner. If consensus cannot be
reached, the deciding maintainer documents the alternatives and rationale.

## Conflicts of interest

Reviewers must disclose material commercial or personal conflicts relevant to
a proposal. A maintainer with a direct conflict should seek an independent
review when another qualified maintainer is available.

## Evolution

Before the first stable release, governance is expected to evolve. Any move to
a steering committee, foundation, or delegated specification authority will be
proposed publicly and recorded through an RFC.
